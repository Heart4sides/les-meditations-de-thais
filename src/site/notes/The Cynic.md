It's the archetype that believes she is undeserving of love and therefore distrusts everyone & tries to earn everything by herself.

> [!example]- [[God\|Deities]]
>  - [[Ishtar\|Ishtar]] ([[Babylonian Mythology\|Babylonian]]) - As the supreme goddess of carnal love, beauty, and sensory pleasures, Inanna commands the romantic realm but approaches her unions with cold calculation. She completely dismisses romantic idealism, viewing connection as a geopolitical battlefield of leverage. When she returns from the Underworld and finds her husband, the shepherd god Dumuzi, failing to mourn her to her standards, she issues an immediate, unilateral judgment without seeking explanation, sentencing him to the realm of the dead. Her pride forces her to keep her true vulnerabilities entirely hidden, routinely using strategic silence and omissions to ensure she remains the absolute arbiter of her destiny, placing her sovereign choices strictly above his.
> - [[Lilith\|Lilith]] ([[Christian Mythology\|Christian]]) - Originally created alongside Adam to share in the pristine pleasures of Eden, Lilith shifts instantly into the Cynic shadow when her personal autonomy is threatened. She completely rejects male idealism, interpreting Adam’s demand for her submission as an intolerable, violent assault on her ego and equality. Rather than attempting to negotiate or express her deep-seated need for true companionate love, her pride dictates that she flee into the desolate wilderness, preferring harsh isolation over a relationship where she might feel indebted. She views subsequent divine peace offerings as a form of celestial blackmail or a trap, locking herself into an exhausting, eternal rebellion where she relies solely on unilateral judgment.
