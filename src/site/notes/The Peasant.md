> [!example]- [[CARDS/Depth Psychology Theory/Famous People\|Famous People]]
>  - [[Greta Garbo\|Greta Garbo]] ([[0. Change Properties/Actor\|Actress]]) - Blessed with one of the most structurally perfect, photogenic faces in cinematic history, Garbo retired abruptly at age 36. Terribly anxious that her physical efforts, strict diets, and skincare routines would no longer guarantee immediate perfection against the natural aging process, she completely surrendered to total aesthetic and social apathy. She spent the rest of her life in New York living like a reclusive "slob." She completely abandoned makeup, wore oversized, frumpy menswear, and hid her unbrushed hair under massive hats. Her public indifference was a carefully constructed shield to protect her from the pain of an imperfect reflection.
> - [[Lady Mary Wortley Montagu\|Lady Mary Wortley Montagu]] (\-) - A brilliant 18th-century British aristocrat and writer, she was celebrated across Europe for her stunning physical beauty. After a severe bout of smallpox left her skin deeply scarred, she tried various medical treatments to repair it. When nothing worked immediately, her low self-esteem morphed into a bitter rejection of vanity. She adopted complete apathy toward her physical appearance, becoming famous for her aristocratic "slob" lifestyle. She openly refused to comb her hair, wore heavily stained, unwashed gowns, and banned all mirrors from her sight, forcing the world to view her as intentionally lazy rather than aesthetically flawed.
> - [[Sisi\|Sisi]] (\-) - As she approached her 50s, she noticed her extreme crash diets and hours of exercise were no longer producing fast, youthful results. Devastated by her losing battle against time, her obsession crashed into profound, lethargic apathy. She entirely gave up on her wardrobe, dressing exclusively in unpretentious black rags. She hid her face behind heavy veils and fans, choosing to live as a lonely, unpolished wanderer because confronting her reflection without instant, perfect results was too painful.

> [!example]- [[Deities\|Deities]]
>  - [[Aergia\|Aergia]] (\-) - She is the literal goddess of apathy, sloth, and laziness. In myth, she is a divine being of high cosmic lineage but chooses to spend eternity asleep, unwashed, and surrounded by stagnation. Her apathy stems from the ultimate divine defeatist mindset: in a world of flawlessly radiating, hyper-active Olympians, any effort she makes to match their active, radiant splendor is completely hopeless. She embraces the "peasant/slob" lifestyle as a protective defense mechanism against her own cosmic insignificance.
> - [[Yuki-Onna\|Yuki-Onna]] (\-) - In many tales, this snow spirit possesses breathtaking, ethereal genetic beauty. However, when she tries to integrate into human society to experience warmth, health, and love, her efforts fail to yield fast results—her cold nature inevitably slips out, ruining her human disguise. Out of deep frustration and hopelessness, she spirals into severe apathy. She retreats to a desolate, messy mountain shack, letting her appearance become ragged and wild, hiding in the snow because she believes she will never truly look or feel "naturally beautiful or alive."
