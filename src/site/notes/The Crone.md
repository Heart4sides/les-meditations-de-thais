The Crone is the [[Shadow Female Archetype\|Shadow Female Archetype]] of the [[The Matron\|Matron]]. 
It's the archetype that Judges silently to avoid accountability.

> [!example]- [[Fictional Character\|Fictional Character]]
>  - [[Charlotte Lucas\|Charlotte Lucas]] ([[2. Coeurs/Pride and Prejudice\|Pride and Prejudice]]) - • The Hypocrisy & Utility Facade: Charlotte explicitly marries Mr. Collins purely for utility, financial security, and a house to manage. She is not looking for a Mother archetype role; she wants a home where she can maximize her usefulness running the estate, managing the kitchen, and organizing the domestic chores.
> • Covert Sabotage via Silence: To maintain her own comfort around her insufferable husband, Charlotte engineers her entire day around inaction and avoidance. She intentionally encourages Mr. Collins to spend hours working in the garden or walking in the fields, staying completely silent about how ridiculous he is. She hoards her own thoughts and opinions, letting him make a fool of himself in front of the nobility (covertly sabotaging his social standing through her compliance).
> - [[Peggy Olson\|Peggy Olson]] ([[CARDS/Depth Psychology Theory/Mad Men\|Mad Men]]) - When other secretaries or coworkers make logistical errors or cross boundaries, Peggy often defaults to the Crone's silent judgment and inaction. In particular, when she discovers secrets about her coworkers or bosses, she hoards that knowledge. By staying silent about mistakes or letting others flounder in their administrative duties, she allows them to fail.
> - [[Petunia Dursley\|Petunia Dursley]] ([[Harry Potter\|Harry Potter]]) - She is deeply reclusive regarding the outside world and judges everyone silently from behind her curtains to avoid accountability. She lies through omission (hiding Harry’s magical heritage from him), shifts blame for all household problems onto Harry, and does the absolute bare minimum for his well-being just to keep her own domestic comfort undisturbed.
