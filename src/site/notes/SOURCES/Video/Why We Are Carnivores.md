## Evolution & Human Biology

- 21:00 Human teeth are not flat. True flat teeth are designed to grind down plant fibers through lateral, sliding jaw movements.
- 22:00 Our unique shoulder anatomy provides a rotational capacity that allows humans to throw objects with immense speed and power, a trait not found in chimpanzees or bonobos.
- 23:00 Humans possess forward-facing predator eyes. Color-blindness offers an evolutionary advantage by enhancing contrast detection and low-light vision.
- 26:00 Reference to Dr. Bill Schindler’s work and his book _"Eat like a Human"_.
- 34:00 Fossil records show a sharp decline in dental health and the appearance of jaw deficiencies immediately following the agricultural revolution.
- 36:00 Anthropological data from fossil records reveal a distinct decrease in both human height and brain size after the adoption of agriculture.

## Carnivore Cultures & Longevity

- 42:00 Analysis of Native American health and dietary history.
- 44:00 Examination of the traditional diet and physical constitution of the Mongols.
- 46:00 Insights into the high-fat, animal-based diet of the Inuits.
- 48:00 Study of the traditional lifestyle and health outcomes of the Masai.
- 50:00 Analysis of the ancestral, animal-reliant diet of Australian Aboriginals.
- 53:40 Historical account from Herodotus: a Persian diplomat tells the King of Ethiopia that Persians live to about 70. The King laughs, stating it is no surprise they die so young if they eat nothing but "dirt" (crops/plants). This matches 19th-century logs by Dr. Salisbury, who documented highly active, physically fit Native Americans living to 100–110 years old.
- 55:20 Chromosomal evidence indicates that human biology is fundamentally designed to reach a lifespan of 120 years.

## Pathology & Metabolism

- 1:02:27 Malignant cancer cells require roughly 400 times more glucose than healthy cells. Maintaining optimal mitochondrial health prevents the metabolic pathway required for cancer to develop.
- 1:13:00 Plants load the body with excessive plant estrogens (phytoestrogens). Conversely, dietary carbohydrates actively block the hormonal conversion of testosterone into estrogen.

## Anti-Nutrients & Plant Toxins

- 1:15:30 Nutrient blockers: fiber acts as a physical barrier that shields food from digestive enzymes. Additionally, soy and wheat generate protease inhibitors, which actively stop enzymes from breaking down protein.
- 1:18:00 Consuming certain plants significantly heightens human skin sensitivity to sunlight, with lime and celery being primary triggers.
- 1:21:00 Analysis of cyanogenic compounds and their toxic impact on the human body.
- 1:22:00 Hazards of nightshades: ancient tribes mitigated this toxicity by carefully peeling potatoes and only eating the inner pulp of tomatoes.
- 1:23:00 Gluten drives intestinal permeability (leaky gut), creating a gateway for lectins, bacteria, and foreign chemicals to breach the bloodstream.
- 1:24:00 Examination of the systemic damage caused by lectins.
