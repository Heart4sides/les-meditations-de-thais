- **Abdicated** – Fleeing from rightful authority and leadership.
    
- **Avoidant** – Dodging difficult decisions, crises, or confrontation.
- **Passive** – Letting things happen without taking action.
- **Unpresenced** – Physically there but emotionally and spiritually absent.
- **Complaining** – Whining, self-pitying, and playing the victim.
- **Hoarding** – Keeping wealth, energy, and resources strictly for oneself.
