The Phantom is the [[Shadow Female Archetype\|Shadow Female Archetype]] of the [[The Mother\|Mother]]. 
It's the archetype that Feels unworthy and therefore rejects responsibilities and falls into a detached melancholy. 
These people commonly act as \-... 

> [!example]- [[Famous People\|Famous People]]
>  - [[Queen Victoria\|Queen Victoria]] ([[4. Corps/Ruler\|Ruler]]) - Following the death of her husband, Prince Albert, Victoria slipped into a monumental, 40-year Phantom state. She retreated into permanent black mourning clothes, becoming completely silent, melancholic, and emotionally detached from the daily lives of her children. While she maintained a massive, rigid imperial fake support structure, her children frequently noted that she was completely cold and unavailable to them emotionally, viewing them with distant judgment rather than maternal warmth.
> - [[Sylvia Plath\|Sylvia Plath]] ([[Poet\|Poet]]) - Plath suffered from severe, clinical melancholia and depression. When her marriage collapsed and winter set in, she slipped into the absolute nadir of the Phantom state. She became consumed by an paralyzing sense of literary and personal imposter syndrome. While she meticulously maintained the functional duties of care—even preparing bread and milk for her children on the final morning of her life—she had completely detached emotionally from the living world, retreating into a silent, frozen psychological landscape before her suicide.

She puts on a frantic, fake support structure of being a "perfect stepmom," but in reality, she treats the boy with a clinical, chilled distance because she is completely overwhelmed by her own misery and an unfaithful husband.

Instead of addressing her daughter’s pain, she relies on a fake support structure of superficial criticisms (like telling Joy she gained weight) because she is too psychically exhausted to face her own deep-seated imposter syndrome.

> [!example]- [[God\|Deities]]
>  - [[Coatlicue\|Coatlicue]] ([[Aztec Mythology\|Aztec]]) - When she becomes miraculously pregnant with the war god Huitzilopochtli, her existing four hundred children become deeply resentful and plot to murder her. Faced with the ultimate betrayal by her own kids, Coatlicue doesn't fight back; she sinks into a passive, terrified, and melancholic trance. She stands frozen, bottling up her sorrow, waiting silently for the executioners to arrive while her unborn child has to speak from inside her womb to comfort her. In this moment, her active maternal power drops to zero, and she becomes a passive vessel of absolute despair.
> - [[Demeter\|Demeter]] ([[Greek Mythology\|Greek]]) - When her daughter Persephone is abducted by Hades, Demeter slips into a severe, prolonged Phantom state. Consumed by a profound sense of melancholia, helplessness, and worthlessness as a parent who failed to protect her child, she goes into total emotional and physical detachment. She disguises herself as an old, powerless human woman, sits silently by a well, and blocks her internal light. Crucially, she maintains a hollow, frozen detachment from humanity—withholding the harvest and passively letting a global, deadly famine starve her worshippers because her maternal spirit has gone entirely cold.
> - [[Frigg\|Frigg]] ([[Odinic\|Odinic]]) - When Baldur is ultimately killed due to a loophole involving mistletoe, Frigg’s vibrant maternal energy collapses into a profound, frozen melancholia. Rather than raging like a warrior, she retreats into her hall, Fensalir (which means "Marsh Halls"), a place explicitly associated with weeping and deep, silent grief. In her depression, she bottles up her sorrow and slips into a state of numb detachment, letting the shadow of impending Ragnarök settle over the realms while she remains emotionally unavailable to the remaining gods.
> - [[Izanami-no-Mikoto\|Izanami-no-Mikoto]] ([[Japanese Mythology\|Japanese]]) - While giving birth to the fire god Kagutsuchi, she is fatally burned and descends to Yomi (the underworld). When her husband travels to find her, he breaks a promise and looks at her decaying, monstrous form. Shamed, rejected, and filled with intense unworthiness and sudden resentment, Izanami slips completely into the shadow archetype. She transitions from the beautiful mother of creation into a cold, distant, and bitter entity of death, vowing to passively choke out 1,000 lives every day to balance her broken spirit, transforming her nurturing legacy into a dark, detached prison.
> - [[Nut\|Nut]] ([[Egyptian Mythology\|Egyptian]]) - Nut suffers under a brutal, cosmic curse from Ra, who decrees she cannot give birth on any day of the 360-day calendar, inducing deep feelings of unworthiness, isolation, and suppression. During her periods of cosmic restriction, she is forced to remain a literal "passive shadow"—stretched out, vast, silent, cold, and distant above the world.
